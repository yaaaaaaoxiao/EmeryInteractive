Crystal Structure Viewer

How to use the viewer

3D model

  • Drag to rotate. Scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Click an atom or site to select it and see its coordinates. Shift+click selects up to 8.
  • 2 selected objects draw the direction between them, 3 define a plane, 4 or more draw a polyhedron.
  • Click empty space to clear the selection.
  • Clicking the same spot again picks the next object behind it.

Controls

  • Show turns atoms and each kind of interstitial site on or off.
  • Appearance: hard spheres or a sparse model, clipping to the unit cell, atom opacity, and polyhedra around each site.
  • Neighbors tool: click any atom or site to see its surrounding atoms. For a site, Grow sphere to fit shows where its r/R comes from.
  • Plane & slice: enter (h k l) and move the plane with the slider or the ↑ / ↓ keys. Section shows only the cut; Cutaway removes everything on one side.
  • Repeat cells extends the model in x, y and z.
  • Camera: reset, or look down [100], [110] or [111].

Every setting works with every other one. The page address always describes the current setup, so it can be shared as a link.
